Detailed Services 01 / 04

What I handle inside an outreach campaign.

Each service has a clear purpose, a practical working process and organized deliverables that the client can review and use.

01

RESEARCH / TARGET ACCOUNTS

Target Account Research

I build focused company lists using clear targeting criteria instead of collecting random contacts.

What I do

  • Define target industries, countries, company sizes and business types.
  • Research companies through LinkedIn, Sales Navigator, Apollo and company websites.
  • Review services, market relevance, recent activity and business fit.
  • Check technology usage when it helps identify a suitable opportunity.
  • Remove duplicates, agencies, competitors and poor-fit companies.

Client receives

Qualified company list Company website and LinkedIn URL Industry, location and size details Priority and qualification notes
02

MAPPING / BUYER ROLES

Decision-Maker Identification

I match the client’s offer with people who have the right authority, influence or operational responsibility.

What I do

  • Identify the most relevant buyer roles before contact research begins.
  • Find founders, CEOs, directors, sales leaders, marketing leaders and operations leaders.
  • Review seniority, responsibilities and connection to the client’s offer.
  • Separate primary decision-makers from influencers and supporting stakeholders.
  • Record accurate names, titles, profile links and role-fit notes.

Client receives

Decision-maker list Job titles and LinkedIn profiles Role relevance notes Decision power and priority levels
03

QUALIFICATION / DATA

Lead Qualification & Data Organization

I review every prospect before outreach and keep the data clean, consistent and ready for campaign use.

What I do

  • Confirm that the company matches the target industry and market criteria.
  • Classify records as high, medium or low priority based on fit.
  • Flag software companies, agencies, partners or unsuitable prospects.
  • Standardize names, titles, company details, locations and profile links.
  • Prepare structured records for spreadsheets or CRM import.

Client receives

Clean prospect spreadsheet Fit and priority classification Duplicate and poor-fit removal CRM-ready lead records
04

MESSAGING / LINKEDIN

Personalized LinkedIn Outreach

I write simple, relevant messages that feel like professional networking rather than an immediate sales pitch.

What I do

  • Review the prospect’s company, role and business context before writing.
  • Create personalized connection notes for the first outreach stage.
  • Write natural after-connection and third messages.
  • Adapt the message angle by industry, buyer role and campaign goal.
  • Keep language short, respectful and easy to respond to.

Client receives

Connection request messages After-connection messages Third-message options Role-based personalization notes
05

FOLLOW-UP / CONVERSATIONS

Follow-Up & Response Support

I keep qualified conversations active with polite follow-ups and clear next-step communication.

What I do

  • Plan spaced follow-ups for prospects who have not replied.
  • Write responses based on the prospect’s question or level of interest.
  • Add useful context without repeating the original message.
  • Keep early-stage communication calm and free from pressure.
  • Record replies, status changes and the next follow-up date.

Client receives

Follow-up message sequence Reply drafts for common responses Conversation status tracking Next-action and follow-up dates
06

REPORTING / IMPROVEMENT

Campaign Reporting & Review

I organize campaign activity so the client can see what was completed, what received a response and what needs attention.

What I do

  • Track connection requests, acceptances, replies and follow-ups.
  • Maintain clear notes for qualified conversations and opportunities.
  • Review acceptance patterns, response quality and lead relevance.
  • Identify messages or target segments that need adjustment.
  • Prepare concise daily, weekly or campaign-level reports.

Client receives

Outreach activity report Response and acceptance tracking Qualified opportunity notes Campaign improvement observations

The working principle

Every message should have a reason behind it.

Research explains why the company is suitable. Role mapping explains why the person matters. Personalization explains why the conversation is relevant.

Relevant before promotional

  6. 06

    Campaign Report

    Share completed activity, response status, qualified conversations and observations.
