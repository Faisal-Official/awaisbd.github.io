Core Principles 01 / 04

Five principles that shape every campaign.

01

Research before outreach

I study the company, buyer role and campaign context before preparing a message. This improves relevance and reduces the risk of approaching unsuitable prospects.

  • Confirm company fit
  • Understand the buyer’s responsibility
  • Find a credible reason to connect
02

Quality over volume

I prioritize accurate targeting and suitable prospects instead of building large lists that contain duplicates, agencies, competitors or low-fit contacts.

  • Clear inclusion criteria
  • Clear exclusion criteria
  • Priority based on fit
03

Natural communication

Messages are simple, respectful and peer-to-peer. Early-stage outreach focuses on starting a conversation rather than forcing a product pitch.

  • Short and readable copy
  • One clear reason for contact
  • One useful question
04

Organized delivery

Research, messages and activity records are kept in a consistent structure so the client or internal team can quickly review progress and next actions.

  • Standard lead fields
  • Clear status labels
  • Visible follow-up dates
05

Continuous improvement

I review acceptance patterns, response quality, lead relevance and campaign feedback to refine target segments and message angles over time.

  • Review what gets replies
  • Adjust weak segments
  • Improve future research
02 / 04

The decision rule

Do not send a message until you can explain why this company and why this person.

That simple rule improves the quality of the list, the relevance of the copy and the usefulness of every response.

Quality Checklist 03 / 04

Six checks before campaign activity begins.

01

Company fit

Does the business match the target industry, geography, size and service profile?

02

Company type

Is it an end client, agency, software provider, partner, competitor or unsuitable record?

03

Role relevance

Does the person have authority, influence or operational responsibility connected to the offer?

04

Data accuracy

Are the name, title, company, location and profile link correct and current?

05

Message relevance

Does the message reflect the prospect’s role, company context and current outreach stage?

06

Next action

Is the next follow-up date, response status or campaign action clearly recorded?

What I Avoid 04 / 04

Campaign habits that reduce trust and lead quality.

×

Random mass prospecting

Large unqualified lists create more noise, not necessarily more useful conversations.

×

Generic copy for every role

A founder, sales leader and operations leader should not receive the same message angle.

×

Immediate product pitching

Early messages should establish relevance and invite a response before introducing detailed offers.

×

Unclear campaign records

Without clear statuses and follow-up dates, suitable conversations are easy to lose.
