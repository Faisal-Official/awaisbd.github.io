Role objective

Create a consistent pipeline of qualified B2B conversations through research, targeted outreach and disciplined follow-up.

My role connects research, communication and business development.

I begin by understanding the target market and ideal customer profile. I then research suitable companies, map relevant decision-makers, verify fit and prepare natural messages based on the prospect’s role and business context.

I also maintain organized campaign records so the team can review outreach activity, replies, next actions and qualified opportunities without losing context.
