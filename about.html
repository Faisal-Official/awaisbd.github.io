My Story 01 / 05

Why I focus on research-led outreach.

Good outreach starts long before someone clicks “Connect.”

I became interested in the research behind business development: how companies are selected, how buyer roles are identified, why one message feels relevant and another feels generic, and how organized follow-up turns an initial response into a useful conversation.

Today, I support B2B outreach by combining market research, company qualification, technographic checks, contact discovery, personalized messaging, follow-up planning and structured reporting.

I focus on relevance over volume. Every campaign should reflect the client’s target market, industry, offer and ideal customer profile.
