Core Systems 01 / 04

Five connected systems, one campaign view.

Each system solves a specific part of outreach while keeping research criteria, lead data, messaging and campaign status connected.

SYSTEM 01

Prospect intelligence

Target Account Research System

Builds a focused list of companies that match the client’s market, company profile and qualification rules.

InputsIndustry, geography, company size, service fit, business type, technology signals
ProcessSearch → company review → fit check → duplicate removal → priority assignment
OutputsQualified company list, company URLs, market notes, priority and fit reasons
ICP Brief→Company Discovery→Validation→Priority Accounts
SYSTEM 02

Decision-maker intelligence

Buyer-Role Mapping System

Connects the client’s offer with the people who have authority, influence or day-to-day responsibility for the relevant business area.

InputsOffer type, business problem, target department, company structure and seniority
ProcessRole hypothesis → profile search → responsibility check → decision-power rating
OutputsRelevant people, titles, LinkedIn profiles, decision power and role-fit notes
Target Company→Role Map→Seniority Check→Qualified Contact
SYSTEM 03

Conversation design

Stage-Based LinkedIn Outreach System

Creates a natural path from connection request to follow-up, with a different message purpose at every stage.

InputsProspect role, company context, campaign goal, outreach stage and previous response
ProcessResearch context → choose angle → write concise message → schedule next action
OutputsConnection note, after-connection message, third message, reply and follow-up copy
Research→Connect→Build Context→Continue Conversation
SYSTEM 04

Technology signals

Technographic Research System

Uses visible technology signals to understand company platforms and add useful qualification context when relevant to the campaign.

InputsCompany website, visible applications, platform categories and target technology criteria
ProcessWebsite scan → tool verification → relevance check → technology note
OutputsTechnology stack observations, opportunity signals and platform-fit notes
Website→Technology Check→Signal Review→Qualification Note
SYSTEM 05

Data and reporting

Campaign Tracking & Reporting System

Keeps prospect information, outreach activity, responses, follow-up dates and opportunity notes in one structured view.

InputsLead records, message stage, activity date, response, status and next action
ProcessRecord → update → classify → review patterns → report progress
OutputsClean lead sheet, activity summary, response tracking and improvement observations
Lead Record→Activity Log→Status Update→Campaign Report
